About Me

My career started in technical support, deploying and supporting the Koha open-source library system across academic library branches in Riyadh — work that gave me a close, ground-level understanding of how library systems actually get used.

That grew into building internal business applications, migrating legacy CRMs to Epesi and Vtiger, and eventually into full backend development. Today, at Progress-IT, I build institutional systems from the ground up: a library portal integrated with Koha over REST and SRU APIs, a clinic management system covering registration through billing and diagnostics, and a B2B pharmacy distribution platform with geo-based warehouse and supplier logic.

I care about backend logic being correct as much as it is fast — things like using integer-based financial calculations to remove rounding inconsistencies in pricing, or preserving discount ratios correctly when order quantities change.
